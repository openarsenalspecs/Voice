## Specification Overview

Beverage Cultures is a modular open source specification for comprehensive beverage knowledge, professional education, cultural beverage preservation, AI-assisted learning, hospitality training, and beverage invention.

The specification brings together bartending, mixology, barista skills, coffee, tea, spirits, wine, beer, cocktails, mocktails, zero proof beverages, beverage science, sensory education, hospitality, business operations, and beverage creativity within a unified framework.

Beverage Cultures is designed to support learners, bartenders, mixologists, baristas, beverage professionals, educators, researchers, hospitality businesses, beverage creators, and entrepreneurs.

The specification uses a modular architecture so that individual disciplines, cultures, technologies, educational systems, and professional specializations can be implemented independently while remaining interoperable with the Beverage Cultures Core.

## Ultimate Beverage Laboratory

Beverage Cultures shall function as an educational and creative laboratory where users can move from beverage knowledge to original creation.

The invention process may follow:

**Idea → Research → Inspiration → Constraints → Formulation → Prototype → Tasting → Critique → Revision → Validation → Documentation → Signature Beverage**

The final beverage shall remain subject to human creativity, experimentation, judgment, and demonstrated practice.
